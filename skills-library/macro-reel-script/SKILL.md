---
name: macro-reel-script
description: Research today's gold, Bitcoin and macro news and write a short-form reel script built for views, with checked numbers and sources.
---

Inputs to ask for: the topic (or "you pick"), the account's voice, and the length (default about 180 words).

1. Get today's numbers from live sources (web search, then open the pages): gold, Bitcoin, the 10-year yield, oil, the next Fed meeting and the odds. Note each number's source and time.
2. Find the contradiction or surprise in the news (e.g. "war and inflation, yet gold is falling"). That's the hook.
3. Write the script:
   • Hook in the first 2 seconds: one shocking number pair or a powerful person doing something unexpected.
   • "Let me explain": one plain mechanism, 2–3 hard numbers.
   • "Here's what nobody sees": the twist.
   • An honest line (what could make this wrong).
   • A one-line close that sticks, then a comment-keyword call to action.
   Short sentences. No jargon without a one-line explanation. About 180 words.
4. Give 3 alternative hooks, and the on-screen text for the first second.
5. List the sources with dates. Re-check any number older than 24 hours.

Never invent a number. If a figure can't be confirmed, leave it out or say "about".
