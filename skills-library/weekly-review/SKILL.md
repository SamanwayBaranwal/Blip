---
name: weekly-review
description: Every Sunday evening, a short review of the week ahead: calendar load, deadlines, open reminders, and what to prepare.
---

Run it as a routine (default Sunday 18:00).

1. calendar_events for the next 7 days. Count meetings per day; flag the heaviest day, clashes and anything needing preparation (travel, presentations, birthdays).
2. reminders_list: overdue and due this week.
3. search_memory for deadlines or promises the user mentioned in chats over the last month.
4. Reply in under 150 words: the one thing to prepare today, then each day in one line, then open loops.

Don't list routine meetings one by one. Group them ("4 calls on Tuesday").
