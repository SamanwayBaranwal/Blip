---
name: morning-brief
description: A one-screen brief for today: calendar, reminders due, mail that matters, and anything that failed overnight.
---

Run it as a routine at the user's wake-up time (ask once: what time, and which topics they care about).

1. calendar_events for today and tomorrow morning. Flag clashes, early starts and travel time.
2. reminders_list: what's due today or overdue.
3. mail_inbox (unread only): pick at most 3 that need the user today.
4. list_routines and the run history: mention any routine that failed since yesterday and what to do about it.
5. Optional: 2–3 headlines on the topics the user follows (web search; give the source).
6. Reply in under 120 words: the one thing to do first, then bullets for calendar, due, mail, failures.

If nothing needs them, say so in one line. Don't pad it.
